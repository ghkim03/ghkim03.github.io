# 🏢 (주) 여기우리 (HereWe Inc., formerly Dasty)

[← Back to home](/)

**Official website:** [dimension.company](https://dimension.company/)

## Employment

- **Period:** May 24, 2022 – December 1, 2023 (converted to full-time on December 1, 2022)
- **Role:** Full-stack developer

## What I worked on

- Built frontend and backend features with Svelte, JavaScript/TypeScript, and SCSS.
- Designed and implemented the database architecture from the initial service plan.
- Implemented 3D avatar animation using Three.js.
- Developed a YouTube video-synchronization feature for real-time interaction between users.
- Continuously improved and maintained the product.

## Experience and achievements

- Spent about 1.5 years as an early-stage startup developer, including the first 6 months as an unpaid intern.
- Lived through a full product pivot — from a "mobile 3D SNS" to a "user-space design metaverse" — and owned the development of core features throughout.
- Helped the pivoted product get selected for the **SeedTIPS incubation program**, contributing to the company's seed investment on the strength of its technology.
- Chose to take a one-year leave of absence from school to focus entirely on development and the product.
- Joined without even a working knowledge of HTML, and grew into a developer comfortable across the stack — from database design to 3D rendering and real-time communication — through 1.5 years of hands-on work.
